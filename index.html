The problem

Picking the best subset of assets to hold — maximizing return while minimizing risk — is a combinatorial choice. Checking every combination by brute force scales poorly: with more candidate assets, the search space grows faster than classical hardware can keep up.

How QuantRisk solves it

The selection problem is rewritten as a Quadratic Unconstrained Binary Optimization (QUBO), mapped onto an Ising Hamiltonian, and handed to QAOA running on a Qiskit simulator.
